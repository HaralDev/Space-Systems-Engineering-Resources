      Real orbits

      Below about 160 km, air drag pulls a satellite down within days.
      0 is a circle, 0 to 1 an ellipse, exactly 1 a parabola, above 1 a hyperbola.
      0° follows the equator, 90° passes over the poles, above 90° goes against Earth's spin.
      The angle from perigee to the satellite, measured in the direction it flies. 0° is perigee, 180° is apogee. Drag to pause and place the satellite.

      Ground track
